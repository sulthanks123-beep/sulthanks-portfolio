Why WhatsApp leads convert

A lead form gives you a name and a number, and then you chase someone who may not pick up. A WhatsApp lead is a person who has already started talking to you. Your team can answer in seconds, send prices, photos and location, and book the visit or call in the same chat. For service businesses in Dubai, that speed is often the difference between a sale and a lost lead.

WhatsApp lead questions

What is a click-to-WhatsApp ad?

It is a Facebook or Instagram ad with a "Send message" button. One tap opens a WhatsApp chat with your business, often with a first message already typed, so the customer starts the conversation.

What's a good cost per lead for WhatsApp ads in the UAE?

In my recent business-setup campaigns in Dubai, WhatsApp conversations cost about AED 2.28–7.74 each. Your costs depend on your offer, audience and creative.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business app or the API?

The free WhatsApp Business app is enough to start. If many people answer chats, or you want automated replies and lead tracking, the WhatsApp Business Platform (API) through a provider is the next step.

Are WhatsApp leads better than lead forms?

Usually they are warmer, because the person chose to start a conversation and you can reply in seconds. Lead forms can be cheaper per lead but often bring more people who never answer the phone.

Can the ads be in Malayalam or other languages?

Yes. I run Malayalam-language ads for the UAE's Kerala community, plus English and regional-language campaigns, so each audience hears the offer in its own language.
