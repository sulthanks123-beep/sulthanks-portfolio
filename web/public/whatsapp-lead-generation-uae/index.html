Who it's for

WhatsApp lead generation works best when your customers need a quick conversation before they buy: a price, a question answered, a visit booked. In the UAE that covers most service businesses.

  • Business setup and PRO services: licence questions start on WhatsApp. This is where I run it today.
  • Education and training: course and batch enquiries. I've used WhatsApp campaigns for admissions.
  • Real estate, clinics, rentals and home services: anyone whose sale starts with "Is this available?"

What's included every month

  • Campaign setup in your own ad account and WhatsApp Business number, with tracking checked.
  • Two or three new creative ideas: short videos with real faces, influencer-led where it fits.
  • Language versions for the communities you sell to, such as Malayalam and English.
  • First-message and quick-reply scripts, so every chat starts with a qualifying question.
  • Daily optimisation: budget moved to the ads that bring serious enquiries.
  • A weekly report on chats, qualified leads and cost, reviewed with your sales team.

What I need from you

  1. Access to your Facebook Page and ad account (as a partner, which you can remove any time).
  2. A WhatsApp Business number linked to that Page.
  3. Someone on your team to answer chats quickly while ads run.
  4. Your offer, prices and the questions customers usually ask.
