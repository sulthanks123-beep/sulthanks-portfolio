What I do for you

Meta Ads look simple: boost a post, pick an audience, pay. In practice most Dubai businesses lose money on them because the campaign objective, the audience and the creative don't match what they actually sell. I set all three up around one number that matters to you, such as cost per lead or cost per sale.

  • Campaign strategy and setup in your own ad account, with the Meta Pixel and conversion tracking checked first.
  • Audience targeting by area, language and interest: Dubai, the wider UAE, or specific communities such as Malayalam speakers.
  • Creatives that stop the scroll: Reels-first videos, influencer-led clips and static ads, tested against each other.
  • Lead forms and click-to-WhatsApp campaigns, so enquiries land where your sales team already works.
  • Daily optimisation and a weekly report in plain English: what we spent, what it brought in, what changes next.

Meta Ads questions

How much should I spend on Meta Ads in Dubai?

Most small businesses can start learning with AED 2,000–5,000 a month. That is enough to test a few audiences and creatives. Once we know what converts, we scale the budget behind it.

How soon will I see results from Facebook and Instagram ads?

Leads usually start in the first week. Meta's learning phase takes about 50 results per ad set, so costs settle after two to four weeks of steady spend.

Do you make the ad creatives too?

Yes. I plan and direct the creatives: short videos, influencer-style clips, static ads and the copy. I can work with your designer or video team, or brief one for you.

Can you run Meta Ads in Malayalam, Arabic or Hindi?

I run English and Malayalam ads myself, and regional-language campaigns for different audiences. For Arabic or Hindi copy, I work with a native writer so the ad sounds natural.

Do I keep access to my ad account?

Always. Ads run in your own Business Manager and ad account. I work as a partner with access you can remove at any time.
