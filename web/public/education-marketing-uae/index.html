Education marketing questions

Which ads work best for course admissions?

Usually a mix: Google Search ads for people already looking for a course, and Meta or Snapchat video ads to reach people who haven't started looking yet. WhatsApp campaigns help turn interest into a conversation with your admissions team.

When should an institute start its admission ads?

Four to six weeks before a batch opens. That gives time to build awareness, collect enquiries and follow up before the start date. I plan campaigns batch by batch.

Do you make the video creatives?

I plan and direct them. Short videos with real students and trainers work best for education; I brief what to film and turn it into ads.

Can you track which ads bring enrolments?

Yes. Form leads, WhatsApp chats and calls are tracked by campaign, and I work with your admissions team to see which leads actually enrol.

Do you work with ed-tech and online courses?

Yes. I've run paid campaigns and lead generation for an ed-tech brand as well as for physical training centres.
