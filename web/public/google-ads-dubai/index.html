Google Ads questions

How much does Google Ads cost in Dubai?

You set the budget and pay per click. Clicks for competitive Dubai services such as business setup, real estate or clinics often cost several dirhams each. A starting budget of AED 3,000–5,000 a month gives enough clicks to learn which keywords bring enquiries.

Should I use Google Ads or Meta Ads?

Google Ads catches people already searching for your service, so leads are usually warmer. Meta Ads creates demand among people who weren't searching yet. Many businesses start with Google for high-intent leads and add Meta to grow.

How long until Google Ads brings leads?

Search ads can bring clicks on the first day. Expect two to four weeks of tuning keywords, negative keywords and bids before the cost per lead settles.

Can you run ads in Arabic and English?

Yes. Campaigns can target English and Arabic searches separately. I write the English ads and work with a native writer for Arabic copy.

Do you also set up conversion tracking?

Yes, before any money is spent. Calls, WhatsApp clicks and form leads are tracked so Google can optimise for real enquiries, not just clicks.
