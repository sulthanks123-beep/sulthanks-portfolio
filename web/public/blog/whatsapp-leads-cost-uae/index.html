What does a WhatsApp lead cost in the UAE?

By Sulthan K S, digital marketer in Dubai · 12 November 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer: in my recent click-to-WhatsApp campaigns for a Dubai business-setup company, each new conversation with the sales team cost AED 2.28–7.74. Remember that a chat is not yet a customer: what matters is how many chats turn into sales.

Key takeaways

  • Dubai benchmark: AED 2.28–7.74 per WhatsApp conversation (business setup, 2026).
  • Measure cost per customer, not just cost per chat.
  • Real faces, the audience's own language and a pre-filled first message lower costs.
  • Reply within minutes: speed turns chats into sales.

What counts as a WhatsApp lead

A click-to-WhatsApp ad is a Facebook or Instagram ad with a "Send message" button. Tapping it opens a WhatsApp chat with your business, usually with a first message already typed. Meta reports the result as a messaging conversation started, and your cost per lead is your spend divided by those conversations.

Where my AED 2.28–7.74 comes from

These numbers come from the campaigns I run as Head of Digital Marketing at Al Aseel Business Hub, a business-setup company in Dubai. The ads are short, influencer-led videos, including Malayalam-language creatives for the UAE's Kerala community, running in Facebook and Instagram feeds and Reels. The cheapest conversations came from creatives in the audience's own language with a real person speaking to camera.

Business setup is a competitive category in Dubai. Your costs could be lower or higher depending on your industry, offer and creative.

For most UAE service businesses I prefer WhatsApp: the lead may cost a bit more, but more of those leads become customers, so the cost per customer is often lower.

From cost per chat to cost per customer

Do this simple sum every month. Here is an example with made-up round numbers:

  • You spend AED 3,000 and get 600 conversations (AED 5 per chat).
  • Your team qualifies 1 in 5: 120 serious enquiries.
  • 1 in 10 of those buys: 12 customers.
  • Your real cost per customer is AED 250. Compare that with what each customer is worth to you.

If chats are cheap but nobody buys, the problem is usually the offer, the targeting or the follow-up, not the ad cost.

How to get cheaper, better WhatsApp leads

  1. Pre-fill a useful first message, like "Hi, I'd like the price for a mainland licence", so the chat starts with intent.
  2. Show a real person in a short video. Faces build trust faster than posters.
  3. Speak the audience's language: English, Arabic, Malayalam, Hindi. Matching language often lowers cost per chat.
  4. Reply within minutes. Set a greeting and quick replies in WhatsApp Business, and have someone ready during ad hours.
  5. Ask one qualifying question first, such as budget or timeline, so your team spends time on serious buyers.
  6. Track outcomes. Tag chats as qualified or sold, and tell your marketer weekly so the ads can be tuned for customers, not just chats.

What you need to start

  • A WhatsApp Business account connected to your Facebook Page
  • A clear offer and 2–3 short video creatives
  • A starting budget of around AED 2,000–5,000 a month
  • Someone to answer chats quickly

Common questions

How much does a WhatsApp lead cost in Dubai?

In my 2026 click-to-WhatsApp campaigns for a Dubai business-setup company, each new conversation cost AED 2.28–7.74. Costs vary by industry, offer and creative.

Are click-to-WhatsApp ads worth it?

For most UAE service businesses, yes. The customer starts the chat and you can reply in seconds, so more leads turn into customers than with forms that need a call back.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API for ads?

No. The free WhatsApp Business app connected to your Facebook Page is enough to start. The API through a provider helps when many people answer chats or you want automation.

How do I track which WhatsApp leads become customers?

Meta reports conversations started. In WhatsApp Business, tag each chat with labels such as New lead, Qualified and Sold, and count them weekly to find your real cost per customer.

How fast should I reply to WhatsApp leads?

Within minutes during the hours your ads run. Set a greeting message and quick replies so every chat gets an instant first response.
