What I do for your store

Most online stores in the UAE boost a few posts, see likes and no sales, and decide ads don't work. The problem is usually the setup: the wrong campaign goal, broken tracking, or one creative shown to everyone. I fix the setup first, then build campaigns that Meta can optimise for purchases.

  • Pixel and purchase tracking checked end to end, so Meta learns from real sales.
  • Sales and checkout campaigns for your best-selling products and new launches.
  • Creative testing by region, language and angle, so each audience sees an ad made for it.
  • Retargeting for people who viewed products or started checkout but didn't buy.
  • Scaling what sells: more budget behind winning products, less behind the rest.
  • A weekly report showing spend, purchases, cost per purchase and what changes next.

Case study: WayHomes

WayHomes is a Shopify store selling home products. I've run its paid social since April 2025. Over that time the store has made ₹6.5M in sales from 13,362 orders.

For a knife launch in October 2026, I split the budget into six Meta campaigns by region and language, with a creative made for each audience. They brought 293 purchases at ₹45–75 each (about AED 1.76–2.91) and 772 checkouts, some at under AED 0.50 each. Meta flagged two of the campaigns as high performing.

These results are from India, where ad costs are lower than in the UAE. The method is the same; your costs will depend on your product, price and market.

E-commerce ads questions

Do Meta Ads work for Shopify stores in the UAE?

Yes, when the Meta Pixel and purchase tracking are set up properly and the ads are built around a clear offer. Facebook and Instagram are where most UAE shoppers discover new brands, and Meta can optimise directly for purchases.

What is a good cost per purchase for e-commerce ads?

It depends on your product price and margin. For a low-ticket home-products store, my recent campaigns brought purchases at ₹45–75 each (about AED 1.76–2.91). Higher-priced products cost more per sale, so we set a target based on your margin.

How much budget do I need to start?

Most small stores can start testing with AED 2,000–5,000 a month. That is enough to test a few products, audiences and creatives before scaling what sells.

Do you work with stores on platforms other than Shopify?

Yes. Any store that can install the Meta Pixel and track purchases works, including WooCommerce and custom sites. Shopify is simply the fastest to set up.

Can you run ads in different languages?

Yes. I split campaigns by region and language so each audience sees a creative made for it, which is how the WayHomes campaigns were run.
